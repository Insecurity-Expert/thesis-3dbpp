STACKR
STACKR helps you fit more boxes into a truck or container. Tell it what you're loading, and it finds the best way to arrange everything — and shows you exactly where each box goes.
Saves a copy you can open offline — no internet needed.
WHAT IT DOES
Finds a good arrangement
Give it your boxes and your container. It works out where each box should go — including whether to turn it sideways.
Shows you the result in 3D
Rotate, zoom, and look at the packed container from any angle. Much easier than reading a list of coordinates.
Lets you compare methods
Try different packing methods on the same boxes and see which one fits more in — no guesswork.
Prints loading instructions
Get a simple, step-by-step loading guide you can print and take to the truck.
WHY IT MATTERS
Empty space costs money
You pay for the whole truck, not just the part you fill. Packing a bit tighter means fewer trips and lower shipping costs.
It's hard to do by hand
There's no simple formula that always gives the best arrangement. Trying a few methods and comparing the results is the practical approach.
3D is hard to picture
People struggle to imagine how irregular boxes nest together. Seeing it in 3D makes it obvious where the wasted space is.
Easy to try, easy to learn
Try a method, watch it pack, see where it wastes space, and try another. You'll build a feel for what works with your loads.
Try it with your own load
Enter your box sizes and container size, pick a method, and see how full you can get it.

How to use STACKR
Six simple steps. You don't need any training to get started.
1
Add your boxes. Go to "Start Analysis". You can type them in one at a time, import a file, or just pick a ready-made sample if you want to try it out first.
2
Set your container size. The default is a standard 40-foot truck. Change the numbers if your container is a different size.
3
Pick a packing method. Four to choose from — DGWO (Quick Pack), MOGWO (Safe Pack), Sequential (Balanced Pack), and Repair-Based (Strict Pack). Each one shows the study name plus a plain description.
4
Run a Quick Test or a Full Comparison. Quick Test does one run of your chosen method — about a minute. Full Comparison runs all four methods 30 times each and takes much longer, but gives you a winner.
5
See your winner instantly. After a Full Comparison, "Results" opens with a big winner banner right at the top — no hunting through tables.
6
Print your loading plan. "Loading Guide" gives you a step-by-step plan you can print and take to the truck.
Things to know
A few important things about how STACKR works
1 · Only 2–3 delivery stops
STACKR can only handle 2 or 3 delivery stops. Real routes with lots of stops, or where you reload at a depot along the way, are beyond what this tool can model. So the "unload order" numbers should be taken as a rough guide, not a guarantee.
2 · "Careful handling" is guessed, not measured
STACKR marks a box as "careful handling" when it can only hold a small amount of weight on top compared to the others. Real fragility depends on the material, packaging, and what's inside — something STACKR can't see. So treat it as a rough guide, not a physical survey.
3 · Speed depends on your computer
The times and memory numbers were measured on a shared cloud machine. Different computers run at different speeds, so don't compare raw times between machines. Compare the methods to each other, in the same session, and you'll be fine.
